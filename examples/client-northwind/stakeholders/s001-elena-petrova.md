# Description

Elena Petrova is Northwind's VP Engineering and the client-side
sponsor of the Platform Migration project (`AS001`, active since
2026-05-01, on the Client side of the table). As sponsor, she is the
executive owner of the migration's business case internally at
Northwind: she is the one accountable for the legacy monolith's risk
profile to the rest of the leadership team, and the one whose backing
the project's technical decisions (`DEC001`, `DEC002`, `DEC004`) and
its steering-level status reports ultimately need. Unlike Marcus Chen
(`S002`), who is delivering the work, Elena's role is oversight and
escalation rather than day-to-day technical direction — she does not
personally own any risk, action, or decision record in this bundle,
which is itself representative of a sponsor's usual footprint: present
in the assignment and governance layer, not in the delivery detail.

She is not currently assigned to the Customer Portal Redesign project,
which has its own, separate client-side leadership in Jordan Blake
(`S004`) — a deliberate structural choice in this bundle reflecting
that the two projects, while run by the same client and partly the
same delivery team, report up through different business sponsors at
Northwind.
