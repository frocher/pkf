# Description

Jordan Blake is Northwind's Product Owner for the Customer Portal
Redesign project (`AS004`, active since 2026-06-01, on the Client
side of the table), and the person carrying most of that project's
day-to-day ownership in this bundle. Unlike Elena Petrova's more
oversight-oriented sponsor role on the Platform Migration project,
Jordan sits close to the delivery detail: owner of both accessibility-
and functionality-facing requirements (`REQ004`, WCAG 2.1 AA
compliance, still `Draft`; `REQ005`, offline cart persistence), owner
of the portal's web application record (`APP003`), owner of the
cross-project dependency on the migration project's Checkout API
(`DEP002`), and owner of both portal milestones (`M003`, design
system finalized; `M004`, public launch). Jordan also made the call
behind `DEC003` — committing the project to a real shared component
library over a style-guide document — and owns the risk that decision
was meant to address (`R004`, inconsistent UX across devices) as well
as the vendor-onboarding risk on the analytics side (`R005`).

That concentration of ownership makes Jordan the single
highest-connected node in the portal project's graph of objects, and
the natural point of contact for status on nearly anything concerning
that project short of its underlying technical execution, which Marcus
Chen (`S002`) advises on in his Technical Advisor capacity.
