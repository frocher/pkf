# Description

Project Management covers the ability to plan, coordinate, and track
delivery across a multi-workstream project: sequencing dependent work,
running the cadence of decisions and risk reviews, keeping milestones
and deliveries honest against their baselines, and generally holding
the whole of a project's moving parts — architecture decisions, vendor
relationships, staffing, schedule — in one coherent picture rather than
letting each workstream drift independently. Unlike `SK001` (Kubernetes)
or `SK002` (React), this is a Method/Certification skill rather than a
purely technical one: it is exercised across the entire Platform
Migration project rather than on any single deliverable.

`SR002` records this need at Intermediate required level and Important
(not Critical) criticality, with a headcount need of one — a lighter
bar than either of the two technical skill requirements in this
bundle, reflecting that the migration project is currently relying on
its Technical Lead (`S002`) to absorb a meaningful share of this
coordination work himself, alongside the architecture and
infrastructure decisions he already owns, rather than staffing a
dedicated project manager against it.
